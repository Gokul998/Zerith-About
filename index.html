Why Zerith

Life runs on too many apps.

Payments in one app, bills in email, files in a drive, plans in group chats, trips across four booking sites. You are the only thing connecting them, and every app wants more of your time.

Zerith brings it into one place: one companion that knows your world and acts on it, so you can put the phone down.

Zerith Core

One brain. Everything connected.

At the centre is Arko, Zerith's voice assistant. Just say "Hey Arko". Every service plugs into it and shares what it knows, so Arko can answer and act across all of them.

What we do

Four layers. One companion.

Everything Zerith does sits in one of four layers, the same four circles in our logo. Each layer makes one part of life easier. Together they lead to the same place: you, with your time back.

01 Money

Know where every rupee goes.

Payments, bills, budgets and subscriptions tracked automatically from UPI, cards and bank alerts. No spreadsheets, no manual entry.

Outer ring · the foundation
02 Knowledge

Second ring · your memory
03 Conversation

Talk, and it gets done.

What we believe

Five principles · keep scrolling
Principle · Clarity

Clarity is a feature.

Removing a step is worth more than adding a button. If it needs a manual, it is not finished.

Principle · Trust

Your data stays yours.

Every connection needs your consent, and every answer shows where it came from. Nothing is a black box.

Principle · Control

You make the call.

Zerith prepares and suggests. Nothing involving your money or your plans happens without your yes.

Principle · Detox

Time back is the goal.

We measure success by how much time Zerith gives you, not by how long you spend in it.

Principle · Calm

Quiet by default.

Good software gets out of the way once the work is done. No noise, no badges begging for attention.

What our logo means

Everything leads to you.

Our logo is four circles that meet at a single point. Each one is a layer of your life: your money, your knowledge and your conversations, rising toward the same place.

The white dot where they meet is you. That is what Zerith does: brings every layer together so your life is easier to see and simpler to run.
